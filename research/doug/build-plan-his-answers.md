# Build plan: Doug's own 1-5 answers become the question screens

Source: `Where U Belong (before AI edits).docx`, his own writing, 2,062 words.
Using HIS wording, not the AI rewrite. His is funnier, and "make it funnier" is his ask.

## Why this fits the app and is NOT the slider that was cut

The cut control was a 0-10 "how much does this matter" slider. His scale is five written
answers you tap - the `opts` question type the app already uses for Size, Rain and Seasons.
One question per pick, five picks, so his own Oct 2 rule ("up to 5, no questionnaire
fatigue") still holds.

## Mapping, phase 1 - his answers carry a direction, so they replace the option labels

| His category | App question | His 1..5 maps to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population | size | village, town, small city, mid city, big city - already 5 targets, 1:1 |
| Precipitation | rain | wet ... dry, 3 targets widened to 5 |
| Temperature seasonality | swing | big swings ... steady, 3 widened to 5 |
| Daily heat and humidity | summer | how much heat bothers you, maps to July temperature |
| Transit | transit | 1 loves the car ... 5 no car |
| Commute | commute | 1 doesn't mind ... 5 hates traffic |

Wording kept as his, minus the dashes. Scoring targets kept from the current build so
results stay grounded in the same data.

## Not changed, and why

- **Inclusivity**: cut. He said so himself, and the data research reached the same verdict.
- **Water answers**: his doc has the transit answers pasted under Water by mistake, so there is
  nothing of his to use. Keep the current three, just renamed.
- **Political answers**: answer 3 is a paste of the cost-of-living line. His Tuesday text asked
  for "red, blue or purple", which is what the current Left / Centre / Right already does.
- **Importance-only ones** (cost, culture, healthy, dating, jobs, air): his 1-5 there is "how
  much do you care". Picking it as a top-5 priority and ranking it already says that. Asking
  again after you picked it would be the redundant screen.

## Phase 2, his headline idea

"A dating app for cities": **do it for someone else.** Fill it in AS your friend, send it,
they answer for themselves, both results side by side. The comparison band already exists for
the receive side; this adds the "answer as someone else" start.
