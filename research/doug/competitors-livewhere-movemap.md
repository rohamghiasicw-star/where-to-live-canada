Both sites read directly on 2026-10-14, not from memory.

| | LiveWhere | MoveMap | Where U Belong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## The honest read

**Doug is half right.** On the surface, "answer a quiz, get ranked cities" is the same shape
as LiveWhere. Anyone glancing at both would call them similar.

**The difference is in three things neither competitor does:**

1. **Canada.** Neither ranks Canada as a destination. That answers his Oct 8 question directly:
   yes, Canada first is smarter, because it is uncontested.
2. **Towns, not cities or counties.** A county or a capital can never return Neepawa, Manitoba
   (23% Tagalog-speaking, pop 5,000) or Two Hills, Alberta (29% German). The app can, and that is
   the "I belong WHERE?" moment he wants people to screenshot.
3. **Social.** LiveWhere sells a private $79 answer. Doug's own epiphany - "a dating app for
   cities", do the quiz for someone else - is the opposite model, and it is already partly built
   (the comparison band).

## The thing to tell him, carefully

**The AI-written 26-category list moves the app TOWARD LiveWhere, not away from it.**
LiveWhere has 27 decision factors and a 9-step quiz. Twenty-six "how important is X, 1-5"
questions is that same shape. It is the generic version.

His own handwritten idea - dating app for cities, do it for someone else, make it funnier - is
the part nobody else has. The AI rewrite and his epiphany point in opposite directions, and his
epiphany is the one that answers his own question.

Also worth noting: every question on the 26-list asks how IMPORTANT something is, never what you
WANT. "How important is population size" cannot tell a city person from a small-town person.
His Oct 2 text already got this right ("limited to 5, in order of most important") - that is
exactly how the app works now.
