Climate

NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, US Climate Normals 1991-2020, monthly. Each place gets one home station: the nearest with a complete record, weighed on distance, elevation, and how much of the 12-month record is real. Elevation is part of that distance and not an afterthought, because the nearest station is often not at the same altitude as the town, and a station a thousand metres up a mountain reports the alpine snowpack rather than the streets. Every place shows which station its climate came from and how far off it is. NOAA publishes these in Fahrenheit and inches; they are converted here to Celsius, millimetres and centimetres. There is no US equivalent of the Canadian bright-sunshine-hours record, so that column is absent rather than invented.

Wildfire smoke

Fire-attributed smoke only, not total fine particulate. This distinction is the whole point: total PM2.5 is dominated by traffic, industry and wood stoves, so ranking on it would call Los Angeles and the Ohio Valley smoky and Idaho clean, which is the wrong answer. A long-run mean, so a single extreme fire year counts once rather than setting the ranking. Coverage is the contiguous states; where a region is outside the source the value is blank rather than a zero, because Alaska genuinely burns and a zero there would be a lie.

The rest

Population, income, home value, rent, age structure, sex ratio, marital status and commuting are American Community Survey 5-year estimates at place level. Home value is a self-reported median, not a market price. Political lean is computed from the 2024 presidential result at county level and each place is placed in its county. County lean is a much coarser instrument than it looks: Los Angeles County is ten million people reduced to one number, and a place inside a big county inherits an average that may describe nowhere in particular. Shoreline distance is measured to Natural Earth coast and lake geometry. Soccer pitches, rinks and places of worship are counted within a 15km drive from OpenStreetMap. Pro teams and rapid transit were checked against league and transit-authority sources, recording where the team actually plays rather than the city in its name.

What is missing

The US census does not ask about religion. There is no American equivalent of the Canadian census religion table, so where this app can say what share of a Canadian town belongs to a given faith, it can only count actual places of worship within a drive here. That is a different measurement and it is labelled as one.

Puerto Rico, the US Virgin Islands, Guam, American Samoa and the Northern Marianas are not in this list. People live there and they deserve better, but the boundary data files them separately from the states and each would need its own inset, so including the places without the map would draw them nowhere.

Residents have been researched for 0 of 712 places. That research exists for Canada and has not been done here yet, so this version is scored on measured data only. Where a number is missing it reads .. and is dropped from the score rather than guessed at. Nothing here is a recommendation. It is a way to argue with a list.
