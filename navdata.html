NAV CANADA is the official source of Canadian aeronautical information and as such, is the owner of the intellectual proprietary rights of Canadian aeronautical data. The data is protected under the Copyright Act of Canada.

    Data Licensing

    Data request subscriptions

    Annual Subscription 

    Aeronautical data products are sold as an annual, renewable subscription under the terms of a commercial data licence agreement. The subscription allows you to access your selected data packs and related updates via the Company’s data portal. 

    One Time Subscription 

    Data may be made available for one-time use for research or consultation purposes.  

    Licencing 

    NAV CANADA assesses individual aeronautical data requests and once approved, provides a quote for provision of the data, as well as a copy of a commercial data licence agreement. Aviation liability insurance is a requirement if the data is to be used for air navigation purposes. 

    Following a signed agreement and receipt of payment, instructions on how to access the ordered aeronautical data via the Company’s external portal will be provided. Delivery is only available via this method. 

    Depending on the use of the aeronautical data, user fees will be payable under the commercial data licence agreement. The applicable fee(s) may also vary in accordance with the commercial data licence agreement.

    Ordering aeronautical data products

    Whether you require an annual subscription or have a need for a one-time only request, please complete our aeronautical data request form and submit it to the email address provided on the form. A customer service representative will contact you with details on the next steps.
